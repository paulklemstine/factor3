## Abstract

An eighth brainstorm subagent attacked the framework's remaining precise gaps:
the aggregation-cost exponent of structural witnesses, exact arithmetic without
the CRT split, correlated-but-structured sample generators, and the alethean.org

## 2. POLYFACT (experiment 329): batching does not change the exponent

P(x) = prod_{a=1}^{sqrt N}(x-a) mod N, built in O(sqrt N) multiplications, has
gcd(P(0), N) = p (first gcd>1 at min(p,q), verified 113/331/1039). The
polynomial BATCHES the sqrt(N) atomic probes into one object — but the
construction cost is EXACTLY the aggregation floor, and sqrt(N) =
2^{(log N)/2} is still super-polynomial. The polynomial form does not change the
cost exponent. Barrier 4: structural-witness batching does not compress the
search.

---

## 3. MIXROOT (experiment 330): Newton cannot reach the mixed roots

4 has four square roots mod N: +-2 and two mixed roots u (u == 2 mod p,
u == -2 mod q); any mixed root factors N. Newton iteration from random starts:
0% reach a mixed root (verified N = 77, 143, 221). The Mobius change
y = (x-2)/(x+2) conjugates Newton to y <- y^2, which never converges mod p/q;
starts either cycle or hit a factor by random divisibility (1/p + 1/q density).
The 4-valued branching IS the CRT split; reaching a mixed cell requires starting
exactly at the mixed root (knowing p, q). Barrier 4/6.

---

## 4. JACWALK (experiment 331): the coupled walk is unhelpful

x_{i+1} = x_i + (x_i/N) mod N applies a single +-1 to BOTH CRT coordinates
(genuinely coupled, unlike rho). Verified: 0 gcd hits (below the M/sqrt(N)
baseline — the +-1 walk under-samples the spaced-out multiples), and the sign
sequence alternates (lag-1 ~ 0). The coupling is real but statistically
unhelpful — structured correlation != exploitable correlation. Sealed like rho.

---

## 5. BERGGRENCERT (experiment 332): the Berggren tree is orthogonal

Primitive Pythagorean triples generated by the three Berggren matrices hit N's
factors at approximately the random density (2818/3621/618 hits vs the 3M/sqrt(N)
baseline, within ~2x). The tree's SLOPE coordinates are orthogonal to factoring's
NORM coordinates (density = random), consistent with the lab's Berggren-tree
orthogonality memory and alethean.org's pkg 694 impossibility framework. The
structured correlation is with Pythagorean structure, not N's divisors.

## 6. Meta-lessons

1. **Batching is not compression.** Structural witnesses (polynomials, graphs)
   batch the sqrt(N) atomic probes but the cost exponent is unchanged.
2. **Exact arithmetic cannot escape the CRT split.** Newton basin-hopping
   cannot reach mixed roots without starting at them.
3. **Correlated generators are sealed, not helped.** The Jacobi walk under-
   samples; the Berggren tree is orthogonal. Structured correlation does not
   align with factors.
4. **Eight rounds, ~40 hypotheses, 332 experiments.** The framework is intact
   and its precise gaps are closed. No poly(log N) algorithm emerged.
