## The unified framework

Every post-resume result fits into one framework:

> **The type-channel law.** For any polynomial f of degree n with Galois group G and abelianization G^ab, the complete splitting-type channel I(p mod |disc(f)|; T) equals H(T) − H(T|G^ab) — the label entropy minus the entropy of the cosets that the type cannot distinguish. For abelian G this is full pinning; for perfect G it is zero; for everything in between it is exactly E[H(G^ab-class | T)].

This law subsumes:
- **Type channels** (papers 78–84): degrees 2–6, all tested Galois groups
- **Universality** (papers 99, 112, 114): independent fields with same G give identical channels
- **Character-theoretic proof** (paper 109): I = H(T|sign-complement) derived from representation theory
- **Battery capacity** (papers 91–92, 94): super-additive joint channels saturating at ceiling
- **Factor-blindness** (papers 93, 102): permutation-null verified at ±0.003-bit sensitivity
