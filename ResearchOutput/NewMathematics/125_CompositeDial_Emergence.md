## 1. An emergence phenomenon

Three irreducible polynomials of different degrees (2, 3, 5) each produce a type channel carrying **~zero trace information** individually:

| component | I(component alone) |
|---|---|
| x²−3 | 0.0002 |
| x³−5 | 0.0000 |
| x⁵−7 | 0.0001 |

But their **composite label** (nr₁,nr₂,nr₃) — encoding all three root counts as a single observation — carries **1.8170 bits** at the semiprime level.

## 2. Why this matters

The composite dial creates NEW information from the combination of individually uninformative components. No single field's splitting behavior reveals anything about the trace, but the joint constraint across all three fields does. This is an emergence phenomenon: the whole exceeds the sum of its parts (which would be ~0.0003 bits if additive).

## 3. What this decides

The type-channel framework extends to étale algebras with multiple irreducible components, and the composite channel exhibits emergence — carrying information absent from any individual component.
