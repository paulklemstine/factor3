## 1. The question the campaign asked

Can the Berggren/Price Pythagorean trees be climbed toward a semiprime's node using
an ENERGY SPECTRUM computed from N alone? The prior record sealed residues
(exp 416/paper 81) but left non-residue channels formally open — exactly the gap the
upstream Catalog's energy-spectrum packages (#526, #761, #822) gesture at.

## 2. The answer: SEALED AT FOUR STRENGTHS

The only real content is the factor-derived ORACLE bound — I(1{d≤B}; b₁) peaks
0.4798 bits at B≈22758 (B*=10420 for ≥90%) — unrealized by every tested probe.

## 3. The exact laws banked

- **Price two-adic law (paper 198):** letter₀=A ⟺ N≡1 mod 4; letter₁=A ⟺ N mod 8 ∈
  {1,3}; first-two A-nesses ↔ N mod 8 BIJECTION; mechanism v₂(p+q)-capped readout;
  death at position 2 provable (zero-variance conditional nulls); which-factor wall
  holds; capacity saturates exactly at modulus 8 in the type-channel family.
- **Berggren parent-interval law** (paper 81, reused): ratio bands decide parents.
- **Ascent economics (paper 194):** restart-from-root E = h·α^(−h) dominates DFS in
  99% of cells; branching base pinned at 3 ∀α<1 (effective branching refuted);
  beating EXACT Fermat requires per-step accuracy α* ≥ 0.85 AND feature cost ≤ 3000
  visit-equivalents; balanced strata never win; the deep-dB tail governs feasibility.
- **Sequential hint taxonomy entry (paper 194):** branch hints COMPOUND (α^dB joint
  success) — speedup diverges where the paper-138 class-hint master law saturates at
  its 1/θ cap. A qualitatively new hint value type.
- **Corrections:** dP slope is 1.211 [1.12, 1.30], not the circulating 1.4; the old
  dB-vs-Fermat-cost anti-correlation is stratum-dependent (−0.36 pooled, +0.44 main).

## 4. Method laws banked (lab-wide)

1. **Row-shuffle nulls are the wrong null for deterministic functions of N** — any
   fine monotone function of N inherits population scale-stratification and flags
   z≫3. Correct controls: condition on magnitude (log-N strata, fine (n,m) cells)
   or test transfer beyond knowing N. (Joins the paper-70 null-design lesson.)
2. **Derivation-first validation**: deriving structures before measurement refuted a
   wrong literature matrix pre-use ((3,4,5)→(6,6,14)) and produced the exact map.
3. **Smoke-run pipeline checks as mechanism detectors**: exp549's check caught the
   zero-crossing ≠ Fermat-hit conflation that had produced a plausible-but-wrong
   mechanism narrative (papers 193/195, retracted same session).

## 5. What would reopen the question

A probe class that is none of the four sealed kinds — genuinely non-residue,
non-Gauss, non-bracket, and NOT monotone in |N| — carrying per-step letter accuracy
above α* ≈ 0.85 at cost ≤ 3000 visit-equivalents. The oracle bound says up to 0.48
bits of positional content EXISTS in the geometry; the seal says nothing cheap reads
it. Recovering the path string from magnitudes would require resolving Gauss digits
of ρ at every depth — the scan again.
